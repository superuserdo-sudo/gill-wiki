System Services

System services are intended to provide functionality outside the minimal kernel layer.

Examples

  • Process or task management services.
  • File-system services.
  • Device services.
  • Networking services.
  • System information services.

These are architectural categories, not a claim that all listed services are already implemented in Gill.
